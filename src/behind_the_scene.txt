POST /api/checkout
    ↓
Creates Order (status: 'created')
    ↓
Publishes ORDER_CHECKOUT_REQUESTED event
    ↓
POST /api/webhook/product-validated
    ↓
Publishes PRODUCT_VALIDATED event to RabbitMQ
    ↓
Consumer receives message
    ↓
Updates order (status: 'pending_payment')
    ↓
Creates order items
    ↓
Publishes PAYMENT_REQUESTED event
